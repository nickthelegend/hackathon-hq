### #1 LEASH: zero-knowledge spending mandates for AI agents
- **Pitch:** let your agent spend money (PayPal, USDC, x402) only inside a secret policy it can't break, even when it's prompt-injected.
- **User and pain:** devs and companies giving agents budgets (procurement, ad spend, API buying). Today the options are a blank-cheque API key or a human approving everything. Prompt injection that drains wallets is the #1 fear (ENShell at Cannes won a finalist spot on exactly this).
- **Why now:** AP2 mandates, x402 at scale, PayBox (MoonPay) launching a credential vault for agents, and the Product Hunt trend of agents moving into execution.
- **Unique:** existing guards enforce *public* rules (ENShell) or prove a model ran (zkX402). zkAPI hides the payer but not the policy. A quick search found nothing that **proves each payment satisfies a hidden policy** across both fiat (PayPal) and crypto rails. This is not proof that nobody has built it.
- **Hard core:** a Noir circuit over a committed policy (Merkle allowlist of merchants, per-tx and rolling caps tracked as a private state note with a nullifier, time windows). It's verified onchain by an EIP-7702/4337 account for crypto, and off-chain by a "Leash gateway" that releases PayPal payouts/orders only on a valid proof. Ships as an MCP server `leash.pay()`.
- **MVP (≈5–7 days):** a 3-rule circuit; one EVM verifier (Arbitrum/Celo/Arc); PayPal sandbox gateway; MCP tool; Next.js dashboard to write the policy (stored only as a hash).
- **Demo moment:** a live prompt injection tries "send $500 to attacker". Proof generation fails and the payment is impossible. A legit $3 purchase succeeds through PayPal *and* a $0.02 x402 call succeeds on-chain, both with proofs. The explorer shows a proof but no rules.
- **Business model:** a per-agent seat plus a basis-point fee on guarded spend; an enterprise policy console; a plugin in the PayBox marketplace.
- **Fits:** PayPal AI Hackathon (Nov 12, $67.5k) https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/ · PayBox Hackathon Ch.1 (no deadline listed, $20k USDC) https://developer.paybox.sh/ · Celo Agents on Open Rails (Nov 9) · Arc Microgrants (Nov 1) https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/arc-microgrants-2 · Midnight Wave 3 (Nov 16) https://app.akindo.io/wave-hacks/jaMZjqPOBsLXvjdG · Arbitrum Open House (Dec 5) https://www.hackquest.io/hackathons/Arbitrum-Open-House-Dubai-Online-Buildathon · DevFest Münster "securing agentic systems" (Nov 21, in person).
- **Why #1:** it's Nick's core strength, there's a fear-driven buyer, it fits 6+ listings, and the demo is undeniable. The ZK part makes it hard to copy.

### #2 BLACKBOX RECEIPTS: a verifiable flight recorder for agents
- **Pitch:** every agent action emits a signed, hash-chained receipt (inputs, tools, permissions, cost, approver, result), anchored on Walrus/chain with selective-disclosure proofs for auditors.
- **User and pain:** teams deploying agents into regulated or ops workflows (DevSecOps, finance) who can't answer "why did the agent do that, and who approved it?" This blocks rollout.
- **Why now:** Product Hunt explicitly names "execution receipts" as an open startup idea. Explainability is a judged track (Open Agent's "The Agent That Can Explain Why"). GitLab and DataHub winners won on evidence gates and verification.
- **Unique:** observability tools (LangSmith-style traces) are mutable and vendor-held. Tamper-evident receipts with ZK/Merkle selective disclosure ("prove the agent never touched prod secrets without revealing the full log") didn't show up as a shipped product in a quick search. The idea itself is publicly suggested, so execution and the crypto layer are the moat. **Uniqueness risk is moderate.**
- **Hard core:** an MCP proxy that intercepts tool calls; a Merkle log with per-step signatures (TEE or device key); a policy checker; disclosure proofs; a replay UI.
- **MVP (≈3–5 days):** an MCP proxy, a receipts store (Walrus), a GitLab Duo flow integration, a timeline UI, one "prove property X" query.
- **Demo moment:** an agent ships a fix. The auditor clicks "prove no secrets read". The proof verifies, then someone tampers one log line live and the chain breaks red.
- **Business model:** usage-based SaaS per receipt; a compliance tier (SOC2/EU AI Act exports).
- **Fits:** Open Agent Hackathon (sprint Oct 22–27, sign-up Oct 20) https://hackathon.genai.works/ · GitLab Transcend (Oct 27, $45k) https://gitlab-transcend.devpost.com/ · Tatum x Walrus Total Recall (Nov 10 17:00 UTC) https://tatum.io/hackathon/total-recall · Decentralize AI (verifiable AI, to Feb 27 2027) https://decentralizeai.tech/ · Cortensor #5 (Dec 18).

### #3 TASTEPROBE: GEO audits using synthetic buyers from a taste graph
- **Pitch:** see how ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini recommend (or ignore) your brand to *thousands of realistic buyer personas*, then get the fixes.
- **User and pain:** DTC brands, restaurants and SaaS marketers losing traffic to AI answers with no idea who gets recommended, or why.
- **Why now:** GEO is a breakout category (ReSO AI #1 weekly and #4 monthly on Product Hunt; OpenSEO in the Top 10). Articos MCP (simulated buyer reactions) also hit the Top 10.
- **Unique:** GEO tools track keyword prompts; they don't build *culturally grounded personas*. Grounding personas in Qloo's 250M-entity taste graph and running multi-model prompt simulations is, as far as a quick search shows, new.
- **Hard core:** persona generation from Qloo affinities; a prompt-variation engine across 3+ model APIs; recommendation extraction and share-of-voice statistics with confidence intervals; a counterfactual "what content change flips this persona" agent.
- **MVP (≈3–4 days, buildable mostly in Bolt):** 200 personas, 3 models, share-of-voice heatmap, top 5 fixes.
- **Demo moment:** "Gen-Z vegan foodies in Mumbai never hear your restaurant's name from ChatGPT; your competitor wins 68%." Apply the fix, re-run, and watch the bar move.
- **Business model:** $99–$499/month SaaS, agency white-label.
- **Fits:** Qloo Agentic Hackathon (Oct 30, $25k plus a possible $25k Calacanis investment) https://qloo.devpost.com/ · Bolt Builders Week (Oct 20, only if a fast version can be built today) https://bolt.new/builders-week · Galuxium Nexus V2 (Oct 31, must be revenue-generating) · Nebius x NVIDIA (Oct 30, "Best Apps and Agents") https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/ · Meta Global AI (apply Oct 25).
- **Note:** this is pure AI with no ZK, so it broadens Nick's profile and pulls on his full-stack skills.

### #4 MNEMOSYNE: user-owned agent memory you rent out per recall
- **Pitch:** your preferences and history live encrypted on Walrus. Any agent (shopping, travel, coding) must ask, and pay per recall via x402, to read the slice you allow, and it gets ZK attribute proofs instead of raw data ("over 18, vegetarian, budget < ₹5k").
- **User and pain:** people re-explaining themselves to every agent, while each platform hoards your memory in its own silo.
- **Why now:** the Walrus Memory hackathon, memory as the bottleneck for agents, and x402/zkAPI rails maturing.
- **Unique:** existing memory layers (Mem0-style) are developer-side and platform-owned. User-owned memory that is selectively disclosed and paid per read did not appear as a shipped product in a quick search.
- **Hard core:** a Seal/Walrus encryption policy; Noir attribute proofs over memory commitments; an x402 metering gateway; an MCP memory server.
- **MVP:** an Expo app to curate memory, an MCP server, 2 consumer agents, a payment and proof flow.
- **Demo moment:** a travel agent asks for "dietary + budget". The user's phone pops a consent request, the agent gets a proof (not the data) and pays ₹0.10. Then revoke, and the next call fails.
- **Business model:** take rate on paid recalls; premium vault.
- **Fits:** Tatum x Walrus Total Recall (Nov 10) https://tatum.io/hackathon/total-recall · Midnight Wave 3 (Nov 16) · Decentralize AI (data sovereignty) · PayBox (plugin) · Solana Encode/MLH (Nov 8).

### #5 SENSORSEAL: payouts on proof of a physical event
- **Pitch:** a secure-element IoT sensor signs readings, and a ZK proof of "threshold crossed for N hours here" automatically triggers parametric insurance or supplier payments, without leaking the raw data stream.
- **User and pain:** smallholder farmers, cold-chain shippers and microinsurers, where claims take weeks and oracle data is trust-me.
- **Why now:** YC F26 has 14.3% robotics/physical companies; Nebius has a Physical AI track; Cannes rewarded physical-world projects; QIE has a "Commerce and Real World" track.
- **Unique:** OctetProof does proof of presence, but a quick search found no open pipeline from device signature to ZK predicate to stablecoin payout.
- **Hard core:** verifying a P-256 device signature inside the circuit; a hash chain of readings; geohash binding; a payout contract; an optional anomaly model (NVIDIA open model on Nebius) to flag tampered sensors.
- **MVP:** ESP32 + DHT22 (or a simulated key), a Noir circuit, a payout contract on Arbitrum/Celo, a farmer dashboard (Expo).
- **Demo moment:** a hair dryer on the sensor makes the payout land within a minute; a spoofed reading is rejected.
- **Business model:** a per-policy fee charged to microinsurers and logistics companies; hardware kits.
- **Fits:** Nebius x NVIDIA Physical AI (Oct 30) · QIE Mainnet 3.0 (Nov 30, 50% of the prize tied to traction) https://hackathon.qie.digital/ · Arbitrum Open House (Dec 5) · Decentralize AI · Celo (Nov 9).
- **Risk:** hardware plus ZK is the hardest of the five to finish in time.
